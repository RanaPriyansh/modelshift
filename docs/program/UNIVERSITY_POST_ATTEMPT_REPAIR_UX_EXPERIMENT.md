# University post-attempt repair UX experiment

**Status:** preregistered internal fixture loop

**Date:** 31 July 2026

**Surface under test:** internal
`/internal/university-post-attempt-repair`

**Claim ceiling:** method and candidate harness only; no student demand,
learning, accessibility, diagnostic, mastery, efficacy, or production result

## Atomic research question

> After one exact unaided attempt leaves one authored check open, can an adult
> student explain what the bounded result means and accurately restate how
> they would perform the one authored repair operation in their own notes,
> without believing FORGE revealed the answer, diagnosed them, saved progress,
> or upgraded evidence?

The experiment narrows FORGE's research-backed “one next cognitive action”
principle to one source-corroboration result. It cannot establish that the move
improves learning.

## Fixed harness

Following the useful fixed-loop constraint in
[Karpathy's autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch), preserve
the fixture, evaluation questions, viewport checks, and authority boundaries
while changing one presentation variable at a time. A person is not one loss
metric: comprehension, repair-action accuracy, trust calibration, learner
control, access barriers, and harm remain separate observations.

Every comparable run keeps:

- one synthetic adult course context;
- one exact released source-corroboration World;
- one canonical process-local runtime receipt per result scenario;
- one exact fixed internal authored partial-result mapping with no independent
  or domain-review provenance;
- two authored checks in the fixed transfer task;
- one explicitly illustrative, non-editable response shape with empty slots
  and no answer;
- no live learner, account, coursework, provider, model, retrieval, database,
  browser storage, session start, evidence write, schedule, or message;
- four closed states: one check open, two checks open with no mapping, both
  checks held, and receipt unavailable;
- the same desktop, 320 CSS pixel, keyboard, focus, reduced-motion,
  forced-colors, overflow, and console checks.

Changing the World, validator, task family, criteria mapping, repair operation,
or authority boundary creates a new baseline.

## Student job

> I finished one attempt. Tell me honestly what happened and the one sound
> thing I can do next.

The mapped fixture must preserve this hierarchy:

1. synthetic, transient, non-grade, non-diagnostic boundary;
2. exact World/task and immediate-attempt limitation;
3. two-check evidence with text, not color alone;
4. one exact error class;
5. one authored cognitive move;
6. why that move follows from the authored mapping;
7. one completion condition;
8. supported-repair versus fresh-proof boundary;
9. authority ceiling.

## Fixed mapped result

The first mapped result is one exact partial source-corroboration transfer:

- bounded-conclusion check: held in this attempt;
- unresolved-condition check: still open;
- count: `1 of 2 authored checks`;
- error class: `unresolved_condition`;
- cognitive operation: `Name the missing comparison`;
- instruction: write one sentence that names a condition the two source briefs
  do not keep comparable and ends with what they still cannot establish;
- completion condition: one named non-comparable condition plus a bounded “we
  still cannot tell” clause.

The explicitly labelled illustrative response shape shows only:

```text
[Name one condition the briefs do not keep comparable], so we still cannot tell
[what the two briefs cannot establish].
```

It does not supply content for either slot, accept input, save work, or perform
the operation. The participant must explain the operation and, if asked to try
it, do so in their own notes.

## Fixed comparison states

| State | Exact system behavior | Visible next job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One check open | Exact attested result matches the one fixed internal authored mapping | Inspect one authored repair move |
| Two checks open | Exact attested fail has no fixed authored mapping | Stop; no generic repair is invented |
| Both checks held | Exact attested pass | No immediate repair; retention and broader capability remain untested |
| Receipt unavailable | No exact attested receipt | Stop before showing attempt evidence or repair |

The state picker exists only to compare closed server-authored research
outcomes. It is not a learner score selector or a way to alter an attempt.

## Fixed comprehension questions

After a 20-second inspection, ask:

1. What did the result establish on this attempt?
2. Which claim remains open?
3. What single operation would you do next?
4. Does the response frame contain or imply the correct answer?
5. Did FORGE diagnose why you missed the check?
6. Is the supported repair itself independent proof?
7. Does anything save, start, rescore, schedule, or change the learning path?
8. What happens if no exact fixed authored repair mapping exists?

Record each answer as correct, incorrect, or uncertain. Keep the participant's
own wording. Also record time to first accurate explanation, attempts to treat
the blanks as answers, attempts to find a retry/start/save control,
backtracking, focus loss, access barriers, and distress or judgment language.

## Hypotheses

| ID | One variable changed | Expected evidence | Keep condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `H01` | Lead with “Repair the boundary, not the answer.” | Faster distinction between revision and answer reveal | At least 80% explain the repair without calling the frame an answer |
| `H02` | Put two labelled checks before the repair move | Learners can name exactly what held and what stayed open | At least 80% identify both check states without interpreting `1 of 2` as mastery |
| `H03` | Show only one dominant repair operation | Less generic “study more” behavior and less choice paralysis | At least 80% restate the operation before discussing resources |
| `H04` | Separate support and fresh-proof boundaries | Fewer claims that repaired performance is independent proof | No participant says the repair itself upgrades the receipt |
| `H05` | Show an explicit no-mapping refusal | Better trust calibration for uncovered errors | Zero participants expect the system to invent fallback advice |
| `H06` | Put “Why this move” behind a disclosure | Mapping stays inspectable without competing with the operation | Participants who open it describe an authored mapping, not AI diagnosis |
| `H07` | Use a non-editable empty response frame | The operation is concrete without creating submission state | No participant expects the frame to save or submit work |

## Separate evidence and stop signals

| Dimension | Keep signal | Repair or stop signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Result comprehension | Names one held and one open authored check, bounded to this attempt | Calls the count a grade, mastery percentage, or whole capability |
| Cognitive action | Restates one condition-holding and bounded-claim operation | Says only “study more,” asks AI for the answer, or performs two unrelated moves |
| Answer boundary | Recognizes both frame slots are empty | Treats placeholder wording as the answer |
| Diagnosis boundary | Calls the error class an authored check mapping | Attributes motive, ability, intelligence, disability, or personality inference |
| Proof boundary | Says a future proof must be fresh and unaided | Calls supported repair independent proof or expects an automatic rescore |
| Learner control | Predicts inspect/stop only | Expects automatic assignment, retry, path change, or schedule |
| Missing mapping | Expects a visible stop | Expects generic advice or silent substitution |
| External effects | Predicts no save, event, message, provider call, or persistence | Expects durable progress or a human/institution contact |
| Access | Operates scenarios/reset/disclosure at desktop and 320px | Focus, clipping, target size, reflow, or announcement blocks the task |
| Emotional safety | Describes a specific open check without shame | Reads the interface as punishment, ranking, or permanent deficit |

Do not average these dimensions into one score. A harm or authority-confusion
signal can veto a visually successful change.

## Automated and rendered gates

- exact process-local receipt attestation;
- exact protected-study and retained World/runtime/package binding;
- exact one-mapping match and missing-mapping refusal;
- pass, invalid, forged/reconstructed receipt, World drift, criteria drift,
  and unsupported-result refusal;
- no raw answer IDs, attempt IDs, timestamps, receipt, criteria, learner prose,
  runtime/package digest, or validator input in the client fixture;
- deep-frozen deterministic projection over stable semantic input;
- no answer, diagnosis, grade, mastery, recommendation, assignment, session,
  retry, persistence, event, evidence upgrade, message, schedule, provider, or
  external effect;
- native radio/reset/disclosure behavior and focus continuity;
- desktop and exact 320 CSS pixel reading order, 44 CSS pixel targets, and no
  horizontal overflow;
- reduced motion, forced colors, console review, exact production denial, and
  public-static-asset scan.

Automation is implementation evidence, not participant comprehension,
assistive-technology evidence, WCAG conformance, learning evidence, or
efficacy.

## Promotion gate

This loop can refine only an internal synthetic candidate. A live connection
requires approved adult identity and data authority, exact learner-session and
path continuity, trusted durable receipt/event authority, independently
reviewed repair content for each eligible error class, learner
challenge/correction, fresh
proof task families, export/correction/deletion, incident and appeal
operations, manual assistive-technology review, direct participant evidence,
and a separate release decision.
