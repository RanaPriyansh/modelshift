## Proposed minimum capture schema

This is a design proposal for research/data review, not permission to capture.
It should remain outside any database or participant UI until approved.

### Allowed record classes

1. `run_envelope`
2. `starter_allocation`
3. `exposure_result`
4. `task_observation`
5. `stop_or_withdrawal`
6. `missingness`
7. `run_disposition`
8. `deletion_or_withdrawal_fulfilment`

### Common identity fields

- opaque `runRef`;
- opaque `starterRef`;
- exact protocol ID, version, document digest, and amendment;
- exact candidate, substitute, Pack P, Pack Q, moderator-packet, and review
  envelope digests;
- assigned counterbalance cell and approval-order sequence number;
- record schema version; and
- accountable operator-role reference.

Opaque references must not embed a name, email, phone, university, course,
account, student number, recruitment source identity, or contact detail.

### Coded observation fields

- task ID;
- surface and pack exposure;
- `correct | incorrect | uncertain | not_exposed | missing`;
- misconception category;
- effect-prediction category;
- learner-control category;
- navigation error count;
- elapsed milliseconds only where approved;
- access-barrier category and affected task;
- emotional-safety category;
- contradiction flag;
- missingness reason;
- stop-checklist ID;
- exposure completion state; and
- denominator inclusion fixed to true after starter status.

Raw quotes, free-form notes, audio, video, screenshots, telemetry, DOM events,
keystrokes, course facts, credentials, accommodations, disability details,
wellbeing details, instructor contacts, graded work, and third-party data are
not part of this proposed minimum. A later approval may permit narrowly
bounded wording capture, but must specify exact fields, redaction, access,
retention, withdrawal, and deletion.

### Invariants

- a starter cannot be deleted from the analytical denominator;
- withdrawal or stop ends capture immediately except for the minimum approved
  stop/withdrawal and fulfilment record;
- `not_exposed` is explicit;
- an occupied counterbalance cell is never reassigned;
- no composite score, participant rank, inferred preference, or automated
  synthesis exists;
- a material amendment closes the cohort and forbids pooling;
- every disposition reports contradictions, missingness, stops, deviations,
  and both surfaces; and
- every record remains invalid until its exact schema and data process are
  approved.
