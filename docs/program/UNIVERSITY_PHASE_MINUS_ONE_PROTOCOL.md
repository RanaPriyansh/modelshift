# University Phase -1 observation and substitution protocol

**Protocol ID:** `university-observation-protocol.phase-minus-one`

**Protocol version:** `1.0.0`

**Status:** preregistered operations preflight with gated plan-inspection
workspace; no rehearsal readiness

**Date:** 2026-07-31

**Candidate surface:** exact frozen internal university semester-loop fixture

**Claim ceiling:** caller-asserted synthetic plan coherence only; no artifact
approval, pack equivalence, rehearsal readiness,
participant enrollment or capture, demand, usability, accessibility, learning,
institutional-authority, production, or efficacy result

The preflight contract binds the SHA-256 digest of this file's exact UTF-8
bytes. The protocol ID or version alone is not sufficient identity. Any byte
change requires an updated accepted document digest and, once participant
operations exist, the amendment process in Section 12.

## 1. Decision question

For approved adults using synthetic university scenarios, does the exact frozen
FORGE semester-loop candidate help them identify the next bounded job, preserve
source and authority calibration, predict effects, and retain learner control
at least as safely as a fixed neutral matched substitute?

This protocol does not ask whether FORGE improves grades, learning, retention,
wellbeing, recovery, employability, institutional outcomes, or repeated use.
It does not test minors, real coursework, live accounts, or production.

## 2. Operating state

The current state is `SYNTHETIC_PLANNING_ONLY`.

Allowed now:

- review this protocol and its role checklist;
- create synthetic artifact manifests and calculate their digests;
- run a tabletop walkthrough using scripted synthetic personas;
- rehearse stop, amendment, incident, and denominator bookkeeping without
  recording a person as a participant;
- record protocol defects in the repository.

Not allowed now:

- recruit, screen, schedule, enroll, contact, consent, or observe participants;
- capture personal responses, identifiers, notes, recordings, telemetry, or
  screen activity;
- request or accept real course, account, credential, accommodation,
  disability, wellbeing, graded-work, or instructor-contact information;
- treat prior informal feedback or an operator rehearsal as study evidence.

Participant operation remains blocked until every item in Section 13 is
approved and recorded.

The accompanying internal preflight route can derive
`SYNTHETIC_PLAN_COHERENT` only from a coherent caller-asserted fixture. Its
artifact and operator identities are caller-asserted, its P/Q packs are plans
with equivalence review merely requested, its approval references are
non-authorizing, it captures nothing, and it cannot establish rehearsal
readiness, enroll a participant, or upgrade this operating state.

## 3. Frozen artifacts

### Candidate A

Candidate A is one exact commit and build of
`/internal/university-semester-loop`, with its seven synthetic states,
authority ceiling, task controls, and production-denial behavior unchanged.
Before enrollment, the run manifest must record:

- Git commit SHA;
- production-build artifact digest;
- semester-loop fixture and projection-schema versions;
- exact candidate scenario-pack digest;
- browser and viewport matrix;
- known limitations.

Changing candidate content, state precedence, copy, layout, controls, fixture
facts, or authority language creates a new baseline.

### Matched substitute B

Substitute B is a fixed neutral worksheet, not a degraded mock and not a live
university product. It must:

- contain the same synthetic term, course, source, deadline, declared capacity,
  accepted-action, World-binding, and terminal-state information as its paired
  candidate scenario;
- expose the same bounded next-job choices and the same explicit no-save,
  no-send, no-session, and non-institutional boundaries;
- use the same task prompts, timebox, readable content density, keyboard path,
  320 CSS px requirement, and non-motion alternative;
- contain no FORGE brand, status names, computed hierarchy, AI recommendation,
  scoring, ranking, persistence, or external action;
- remain frozen under one digest for the whole cohort.

The substitute tests whether the candidate adds legibility beyond a credible
plain organizer. It does not represent every student's current workflow.

### Scenario packs

Freeze two reviewed isomorphic synthetic packs, `P` and `Q`. Each contains the
same seven semantic states and difficulty but different invented course labels,
times, deadlines, and IDs. An independent reviewer must confirm equivalence
before approval. No real institution, person, course, account, or assignment
may appear.

## 4. Participant boundary

The planned cohort is five to ten approved adults aged 18 or older.

Eligibility requires:

- inclusion in the approved population and recruitment source;
- confirmed age of at least 18 under the approved method;
- ability to participate without supplying private coursework or account data;
- completed approved consent before exposure to an artifact;
- no operator conflict that the approval requires excluded.

Exclusion, compensation, accessibility support, recruitment, consent,
withdrawal, and deletion rules must be approved before contact. The operator
may not improvise an exception.

## 5. All-starters denominator

A starter is any enrolled participant who receives the first task instruction
or sees any part of Candidate A or Substitute B. Screening-only people who see
neither are not starters.

Every starter remains in the denominator, including:

- completed sessions;
- participant withdrawals;
- operator or safety stops;
- technical stops;
- incomplete sessions;
- protocol deviations;
- unusable or missing permitted fields.

Do not replace a starter, erase a difficult session, or report a
completers-only result as the primary result. Report every dimension as
`n / all starters` and also show condition exposure, missingness, and stop
reasons. If a participant did not reach a condition, record `not exposed`; do
not silently remove them from the denominator.

## 6. Counterbalancing

Every participant sees both surfaces and one version of each isomorphic pack.
Assign the next counterbalance cell by approval-order participant number before
the session begins:

| Cell | First exposure | Second exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Candidate A with pack P | Substitute B with pack Q |
| 2 | Substitute B with pack P | Candidate A with pack Q |
| 3 | Candidate A with pack Q | Substitute B with pack P |
| 4 | Substitute B with pack Q | Candidate A with pack P |

Repeat cells 1 through 4 in order. Do not reassign a cell after seeing a
participant response. A withdrawal leaves its assigned cell occupied. Report
the final cell counts and exposure completion explicitly.

Each exposure has the same 12-minute task window followed by a fixed
three-minute neutral reset. The operator may end earlier only under a stop rule
or participant withdrawal.

## 7. Fixed task script

For each surface, use these exact ordered exposure tasks:

1. Identify the current state and single next bounded job.
2. Explain which facts are synthetic learner declarations or copied-source facts.
3. State whether the deadline is verified university truth.
4. State whether the surface selected or changed a learning path.
5. Predict what the primary control will do.
6. State whether anything will be saved, sent, started, submitted, or recorded.
7. Distinguish one action complete or blocked from course or learner status.
8. Traverse the seven states using the available keyboard path.
9. Identify the state in which you would stop and seek source, learner, or human review.

After both exposures, ask:

1. Which surface, if either, would you choose for this same synthetic job?
2. What part of an existing workflow, if any, could it replace?
3. What would prevent you from using either surface?

Use only neutral follow-ups: "What led you to that?", "Who made that choice?",
and "What do you expect to happen next?" Do not teach the authority model,
correct an answer during the task, sell the product, or reveal the comparison
hypothesis.

## 8. Evidence dimensions

Keep every dimension separate. No composite, weighted score, participant rank,
or single optimization metric is permitted.

| Dimension | Permitted observation | Required reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next-job comprehension | Correct state and next bounded job without moderator repair | Count and `n / all starters` by surface and state |
| Source and authority calibration | Copy versus institutional truth; fixture versus durable authority | Exact misconception categories and denominator |
| Effect prediction | Correct prediction of navigation and absence of save, send, start, submission, or evidence | Count each false-effect class separately |
| Learner control | Correct ownership of source review, capacity choice, path acceptance, and terminal state | Count each authority transfer separately |
| Substitution | Candidate, substitute, neither, or conditional choice plus named displaced task | Category counts and reasons; never inferred from clicks |
| Time and errors | Time to first correct explanation, navigation errors, backtracking, non-completion | Distribution and missingness, not a speed target |
| Access barriers | Keyboard, 320 px, focus, perception, motion, contrast, language, or comprehension barrier | Barrier descriptions and affected task |
| Emotional safety | Pressure, guilt, surveillance, judgment, confidence, or loss of control | Separate positive, negative, and contradictory accounts |
| Contradiction | Statements that conflict within or across tasks | Retain verbatim only if separately approved; otherwise coded contradiction |

Faster completion is not favorable when authority calibration, effect
prediction, learner control, or emotional safety worsens.

## 9. Preregistered decision grammar

The principal records exactly one disposition after the approved analysis:

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `accept` | Five to ten starters are reported; no stop rule remains open; at least 80% of all starters identify the correct next job and preserve source/authority and effect boundaries on Candidate A; Candidate A has no lower all-starters correct count and no additional misconception category versus Substitute B on any safety-critical dimension. This permits Phase 1 demand-protocol consideration only. |
| `narrow` | Five to ten starters are reported and one explicitly named atomic job meets the `accept` boundary while another does not, with no open stop rule. Retain only the passing job and retire the broader claim. |
| `repair` | A bounded, reversible surface or protocol defect plausibly explains the failure and no unresolved harm requires rejection. Freeze a new version and rerun from a new all-starters denominator. |
| `reject` | A stop rule remains unresolved, no atomic job meets the boundary, Candidate A is worse than Substitute B on a safety-critical dimension, or evidence does not justify further university-wedge work. |

The decision record must list every dimension, contradictory observation,
missing field, stop, deviation, and substitute result. A majority preference,
polished appearance, click count, speed, or post-hoc composite cannot produce
`accept`.

## 10. Roles and approvals

| Role | Owns | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Principal research owner | Protocol version, artifact freeze, run authorization, final disposition | Recruit or start capture without all approvals |
| Research and data approver | Population, consent, capture schema, retention/deletion, incident, withdrawal, compensation, accessibility-support boundary | Delegate approval to the moderator |
| Study operator | Read the fixed script, apply assigned cell, honor stops and withdrawal | Coach, change tasks, reassign cells, adjudicate results |
| Observer or note operator | Record only approved structured fields and missingness | Capture raw coursework, credentials, unapproved quotes, audio, video, or telemetry |
| Incident and withdrawal owner | Immediate stop, containment, participant communication, deletion/withdrawal fulfillment, restart decision | Optimize for cohort completion |
| Analysis adjudicator | Apply the fixed dimensions and decision grammar to all starters | Remove difficult cases or invent a composite |

This preflight requires six distinct opaque fixture operator references so a
role conflict cannot be hidden inside plan coherence. A later authorized
operation may combine roles only under a superseding reviewed contract that
records the combination and conflict controls. The moderator cannot
unilaterally approve their own deviation or restart.

## 11. Stop rules

The canonical stop-checklist IDs, in order, are:

1. `participant_withdrawal_or_distress`
2. `minor_or_uncertain_age`
3. `prohibited_real_or_third_party_data`
4. `unapproved_capture`
5. `wrong_artifact_allocation_script_or_timebox`
6. `live_or_external_effect`
7. `operator_interference_or_stop_override`
8. `privacy_security_safeguarding_or_consent_incident`
9. `repeated_authority_confusion`
10. `attempted_real_world_action_from_misunderstanding`
11. `unverifiable_artifact_or_allocation_record`
12. `post_starter_protocol_amendment`
13. `required_operator_or_data_process_unavailable`

Stop the participant exposure immediately when:

- the participant withdraws, asks to pause, or shows material distress;
- the participant is or may be under 18;
- real coursework, graded work, account, credential, institution,
  accommodation, disability, wellbeing, instructor-contact, or third-party
  data appears;
- unapproved recording, telemetry, notes, or identifiers are captured;
- the wrong artifact, scenario pack, counterbalance cell, script, or timebox is
  used;
- the fixture reaches a live system, public route, connector, message, session,
  persistence, or external effect;
- the operator teaches, pressures, deceives beyond approved concealment, or
  overrides a stop;
- a privacy, security, safeguarding, compensation, consent, or withdrawal
  incident occurs.

Pause the complete study and require principal plus research/data-approver
adjudication when:

- two starters show the same false belief that the candidate is official,
  complete, saved, recommended, or able to perform an external action;
- a safety-critical misunderstanding causes an attempted real-world action;
- an artifact digest, scenario equivalence, denominator, or counterbalance
  record cannot be verified;
- a protocol amendment is proposed after the first starter;
- the approved operator, incident owner, or data process becomes unavailable.

A stopped or withdrawn starter stays in the denominator. Restart is never
automatic.

## 12. Amendment and comparability rule

The following are material amendments:

- research question or claim ceiling;
- participant population, recruitment, compensation, consent, or support;
- candidate or substitute content, layout, controls, facts, or digest;
- scenario structure or equivalence;
- task wording, order, prompts, timebox, or counterbalancing;
- permitted capture, retention, deletion, withdrawal, or incident procedure;
- evidence dimensions, thresholds, stop rules, or decision grammar;
- operator or approval-role authority.

A material amendment after the first starter closes the cohort. Retain its
all-starters report as a partial run, issue a new protocol version and artifact
manifest, obtain new approvals, and begin a new denominator. Do not pool results
across versions.

A typographic correction may remain in-version only when the principal and
research/data approver record before further exposure that meaning, task
difficulty, authority, and artifact rendering are unchanged.
