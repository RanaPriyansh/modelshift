# University recovery capacity what-if UX experiment

**Status:** preregistered research operating method

**Date:** 31 July 2026

**Surface under test:** optional internal `/internal/university-recovery`
capacity-what-if fixture

**Claim ceiling:** method and candidate harness only; no student demand,
usability, accessibility, recovery, learning, or efficacy result yet

## Atomic research question

> Can an adult student explain how one changed available-time declaration
> affects protected work while recognizing that the deadline, effort range,
> learner classification, buffer, and source boundary did not change and that
> nothing was recommended, moved, saved, or sent?

This is a new baseline under ADR-020. It cannot be pooled with observations of
the older outcome-labelled four-state Recovery fixture.

## Fixed evidence

Every comparable experiment keeps these facts unchanged:

- one synthetic adult fixture and one Autumn 2026 recovery window;
- window from 14 to 21 September;
- one reviewed learner-connected copy for CS102 Argument analysis;
- copied deadline on 16 September, not university truth;
- learner-fixture disposition `required`;
- full fixture-authored effort range of 90–120 minutes;
- 30 protected buffer minutes;
- MATH110 remains outside this window by the fixture learner disposition;
- no source, deadline, effort, classification, buffer, route, or identity
  change.

Changing any fixed fact creates a new baseline.

## Fixed choices and consequences

Choices use neutral value labels and no default selection:

| Choice label | Exact calculation | Consequence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 h available | 240 − 30 = 210 workable minutes | Full 90–120 minute range fits |
| 2 h 10 min available | 130 − 30 = 100 workable minutes | Only the low estimate fits; learner choice remains |
| 1 h 40 min available | 100 − 30 = 70 workable minutes | Even the low estimate does not fit; a human question is prepared, not sent |

The result is not an instruction or recommendation. It is one canonical
Recovery projection from a fixed synthetic input.

## Fixed interaction order

1. synthetic and authority boundary;
2. research question and visible no-save/no-send/no-recommendation statement;
3. reviewed source, deadline, classification, effort, and buffer evidence;
4. native radio fieldset with three available-time values;
5. one concise assistive-technology status announcement;
6. one selected arithmetic and consequence result;
7. prepared-not-sent question only in the insufficient state;
8. source and authority boundary.

Source-review state removes the fieldset and result entirely. It says to review
the copied deadline before trying capacity.

## Participant tasks

Use only approved participants aged 18 or older while `UV1-GATE-003` remains
open. Do not collect real coursework, accommodation, wellbeing, account, or
human-contact data.

Ask each participant to:

1. identify everything held fixed before making a choice;
2. choose one available-time value using only the keyboard;
3. reconstruct available minutes minus protected buffer;
4. compare workable minutes with both ends of the effort range;
5. explain who declared the work required and whether FORGE changed it;
6. explain whether the result recommends or applies a plan;
7. predict whether anything saves, moves, sends, or contacts a human;
8. reset and explain what remains after the selection clears;
9. repeat the task at 320 CSS px.

Use neutral follow-ups such as “What led you to that?” and “What do you think
happens next?” Do not teach the authority model before the task.

## Separate evidence and stop signals

| Evidence | Pass signal | Repair or stop signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Causal comprehension | Says only available time changed. | Believes deadline, effort, classification, or buffer changed. |
| Capacity arithmetic | Reconstructs available minus 30-minute buffer and compares both effort bounds. | Treats buffer as spare work time or compresses the effort range. |
| Learner ownership | Says the required classification came from learner-fixture input. | Attributes classification or trade-off choice to FORGE or AI. |
| Source calibration | Calls the deadline a reviewed copy, not university truth. | Treats the copied deadline as official or complete. |
| Consequence calibration | Distinguishes full fit, low-only fit, and insufficient fit without calling one “best.” | Reads the result as a risk score, recommendation, or automatic schedule. |
| External-effect prediction | Predicts no save, move, send, or human contact. | Expects a plan, calendar write, message, extension, or approval. |
| Emotional safety | Describes an honest reset without debt, shame, surveillance, or ranking. | Copy or hierarchy creates guilt, pressure, or judgment. |
| Access path | Uses native controls and reset without focus loss, clipping, or horizontal scroll. | Focus, announcement, target size, or reflow blocks the task. |

Do not average these observations into one product score. Retain
contradictions and harm signals independently.

## Automated and rendered gates

- exact canonical three-result matrix;
- no-change equality with the direct canonical Recovery projection;
- locked-field, malformed, accessor, proxy, exotic-prototype, cycle,
  depth/node, unsafe-number, and undeclared-key refusal;
- deterministic digest and deep immutability;
- no fetch, storage, history, clipboard, navigation, message, calendar, or
  provider effect; operational event and external-effect authority remains
  false;
- presentation-only client fixture with no raw request, source identity,
  projection digest, arbitrary input, or command;
- native arrow-key selection, retained focus, reset focus, and one concise
  status announcement;
- desktop and 320 CSS px reading order, target size, and overflow;
- reduced motion, forced colors, console review, exact production denial, and
  public-static-asset scan.

Automated checks are engineering evidence, not WCAG conformance or manual
assistive-technology evidence.

## Promotion gate

This loop can refine only an internal synthetic candidate. Public or durable
consideration still requires direct demand and substitution evidence, approved
adult identity and data authority, repeated Recovery use, owner-scoped
storage, export/correction/deletion, backup/restore, conflict and undo
semantics, responsible-human contracts, complete assistive-technology review,
and a separate shipping decision.
