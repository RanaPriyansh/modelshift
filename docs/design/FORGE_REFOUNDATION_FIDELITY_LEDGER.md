## Above-fold copy comparison

Concept lead: “FORGE maps a credible path from your goal to real capability—through focused work, projects, and proof that matters.”

Implemented lead: “Forge turns a real goal into focused work and evidence that distinguishes supported practice from what you can do on your own.”

The implementation is intentionally more precise: projects are not yet released as a general product capability, and supported practice is explicitly separated from independent evidence.

## Intentional deviations

- The concept microphone affordance is omitted. Voice input is unavailable, no microphone permission is requested, and the command dialog states that boundary.
- Broad generated paths and checkout UI are not simulated. Unknown goals remain non-runnable open questions; pricing says no plan is for sale.
- Testimonials and outcome claims are omitted because there is no source-backed production evidence for them.
- The current build names four reviewed Worlds and does not represent that set as a complete curriculum.
