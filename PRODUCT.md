# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The immediate product focus is adult university students managing a real term across inconsistent course systems, documents, calendars, messages, study tools, and human support. The first research cohort remains provisional and is not yet recruited or validated.

The long-range product serves learners across ages and contexts under age-appropriate authority. The university interface and operating model must not be stretched unchanged onto children or other learning contexts.

## Product Purpose

FORGE helps a learner turn a real question, course obligation, or practical goal into a source-aware next action, active learning or work, protected checking after help leaves, and bounded evidence of what happened.

For the university-first wedge, success means making the student’s current course reality easier to inspect and act on without replacing the university, fabricating completeness, or turning AI-assisted output into a capability claim.

## Positioning

FORGE is a learner-owned learning and degree companion that reconciles the student’s existing systems while keeping source authority, uncertainty, assistance, and independent evidence visible. It is not another course-material generator, planner dashboard, homework answer engine, or AI companion.

## Operating Context

Students already work across an LMS, syllabus, calendar, email, notes, documents, group messages, campus services, and AI tools. FORGE must reduce this reconciliation burden rather than ask the student to maintain another complete academic database.

The candidate first loop is one course deep and all current courses shallow:

1. Inspect what connected learner-controlled sources say and what they do not cover.
2. Review copied facts, duplicates, staleness, and conflicts.
3. Choose or revise one realistic next action.
4. Do active study or real work.
5. Remove instructional help for a named protected check.
6. Preserve an honest result, non-result, or later return.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current accepted implementation includes a fixture-only, side-effect-free manual and ICS-derived course-source candidate boundary.
- The course-source boundary can preserve coverage, freshness, learner decisions, exact duplicates, deterministic conflicts, and restricted assessment policy.
- Learner confirmation proves only that a fact matches the connected copy. It does not establish source authenticity, institutional completeness, tenancy, rights enforcement, or policy authorization.
- Live LMS connectors, institutional credentials, private durable course storage, real student data, generative course tutoring, and deployment are not authorized by this product record.
- AI may propose, compare, rephrase, and direct attention inside strict boundaries. Deterministic code, reviewed sources, or accountable humans own permissions, transitions, factual authority, publication, proof, and evidence.
- Product states must preserve unknown, partial, stale, conflicting, denied, unavailable, contaminated, not tested, and recovery conditions.
- Open decisions include launch-cohort demand, setup tolerance, repeated use, pricing, institutional integration, and whether the first enduring acquisition wedge is source reconciliation, overload recovery, or protected concept learning.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is FORGE Learning OS. The voice is calm, direct, specific, and honest about authority. Existing product language includes “Learner acts. AI assists. Evidence decides.”

FORGE must not use a persistent AI character, chatbot-first shell, generic card-grid dashboard, streaks, points, badges, leaderboards, mastery percentages, emotional attachment, or unsupported success claims.

## Product Principles

1. Reconcile before recommending.
2. The learner reviews and acts before AI performs the protected operation.
3. Source coverage and uncertainty stay visible at the moment of decision.
4. One sound next action matters more than a comprehensive dashboard.
5. Help should become quieter as the learner becomes more capable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Required product verification includes 320 CSS px, keyboard-only operation, visible focus, semantic control names, reduced motion, forced colors, scalable text, low-bandwidth fallback, and complete failure and recovery states. Screen-reader and representative-user evidence must remain explicitly unrun until those sessions actually occur.
